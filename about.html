Our Purpose

Building A Global Walking Economy

Our Vision

To make FITP an everyday, valuable programmable loyalty point across the African continent. We envision a future where your daily physical movement can offset essential living costs and create meaningful economic opportunity.

Our Mission

As a social impact enterprise, FitPesa is dedicated to providing financial inclusion and opportunities to everyone, at any stage of their adult life. We reward movement, connect local communities and make everyday essentials more accessible.

Empowering the Hyperlocal Ecosystem

Through our peer-to-peer PesaMarket and dropshipping infrastructure, we give users access to a customer base that traditional e-commerce costs would normally prohibit.

The Stock Now Pay Later (SNPL) initiative uses existing Spaza businesses as a crowd-sourced methodology to distribute goods, empowering local vendors and strengthening the communities they serve.

Maximum Points Supply

80,000,000,000

The FITP ecosystem is strictly capped at 80 billion programmable loyalty points to ensure long-term sustainability across the continent.
