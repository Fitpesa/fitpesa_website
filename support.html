How do I earn FITP reward points?

You can earn FITP through daily step tracking, daily app check-ins, engaging on FitLovers, referring verified friends, and interacting with rewarded brand announcements.

Are FITP tokens a cryptocurrency or financial asset?

No. FITP tokens are non-volatile, programmatically capped in-app utility and loyalty reward points designed strictly for platform features and marketplace redemption. They are not speculative crypto assets or traded securities.

Is there a minimum balance requirement for payout conversions?

Yes. Users must maintain a minimum account balance threshold of 1,000 FITP before requesting third-party payout transfers or marketplace redemptions.

Which payout mechanisms and regions are supported?

Supported payout channels include Nigeria (OPay, Palmpay, Bank Transfer), Uganda (Airtel Money / MTN MoMo), and Kenya/Tanzania (M-Pesa). Southern African users can redeem points directly in the PesaMarket marketplace.

Why is my withdrawal or payout request delayed?

Payout requests rely on regulated local payment processors. Delays (typically resolved within 24 to 72 hours) can occur due to mobile network congestion, bank compliance verification, or processor maintenance windows.

How do I delete my account or purge personal step data?

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store privacy policies, you can delete your account inside the app under Profile Settings → Privacy & Security, or visit our online Data Deletion Request Portal.
