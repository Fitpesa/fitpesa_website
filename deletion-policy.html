In-App Account Deletion

You can also trigger account deletion directly inside the FitPesa mobile application:

  1. Open the FitPesa App on your device.
  2. Go to Profile Settings $\rightarrow$ Privacy & Security.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm your passcode/OTP.

Data Handling & Retention

Upon account deletion:

  • Deleted Immediately: Name, email, mobile number, step/location logs, and avatar profiles.
  • Retained Data: Anonymized transaction records required for tax, financial auditing, or fraud prevention compliance (held for up to 5 years as required by financial regulations).
