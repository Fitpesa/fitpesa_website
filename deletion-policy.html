Data Privacy & Control

Account & Data Deletion Request

FitPesa is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play policies and POPIA data rights. You have full control to request account deletion and personal data removal at any time.

Submit Online Deletion Request

Use this form if you can't delete your account from the app. We'll contact you at the email or phone number you give to confirm the account is yours, then complete your request within 30 days.

You can also email fitpesa@fitpesa.org with the subject “Delete my account” from the email address on your FitPesa account.

In-App Account Deletion

You can also trigger account deletion directly inside the FitPesa mobile application:

  1. Open the FitPesa app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete.
  4. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Data Handling & Retention

Upon account deletion:

  • Deleted: Name, email, mobile number, profile photo, step history, posts, comments, chats and identity-verification documents. In-app deletion is immediate; web requests are completed within 30 days of confirming the account is yours.
  • Retained Data: Anonymized transaction records required for tax, financial auditing, or fraud prevention compliance (held for up to 5 years as required by law).
